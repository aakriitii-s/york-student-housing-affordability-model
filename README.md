# York Student Housing Affordability Stress Test & Income Sensitivity Model

## Overview

This project analyses the affordability of one-bedroom student accommodation in York under different income and employment scenarios.

The model examines how rent levels, income sources and employment constraints affect students' residual income and housing affordability.

## Research Question

How does student housing affordability change under different income, employment and rental cost scenarios?

## Scenarios Analysed

The model considers multiple student circumstances, including:

- Domestic students
- International students
- Unemployed students
- Working students

The analysis incorporates differences in available income and employment constraints between scenarios.

## Methodology

The model combines rental market data with income assumptions to assess housing affordability.

Key components include:

- Rental cost analysis
- Income sensitivity analysis
- Maintenance support assumptions
- Student employment scenarios
- Visa work-hour constraints
- Hourly wage assumptions
- Residual income analysis
- Affordability stress scoring
- Rent inflation projections

## Affordability Benchmark

Housing costs are evaluated against a 30% housing-cost benchmark to assess the relative affordability of different scenarios.
